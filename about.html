About Weatherly

Making weather information simple, useful and accessible.

🌤️

Welcome to Weatherly

Weatherly is a weather information website designed to help users check current weather conditions and upcoming forecasts for locations around the world.

Users can search for a city, check their current location, view temperature, humidity, wind speed, UV index and explore upcoming weather conditions.

What We Offer

Current temperature and feels-like readings.

Search locations across different countries.

📅

Forecast

Check the weather around your current location.
